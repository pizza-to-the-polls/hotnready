What the "Leave site?" warning feels like

A fake, 100%-client-side demo for the polls.pizza team — no data leaves your browser.

The idea under consideration: once someone has uploaded a photo but hasn't hit submit yet, we add a beforeunload handler so the browser itself warns them if they're about to lose that tab. Try it below — upload a photo (any image on your computer works, it's only shown locally), then try to leave.

↑ Roughly what the real prompt looks like (Chrome, desktop). Every browser writes its own wording.
  • 🧾 We can't customize the text. Browsers ignore custom messages and show their own generic "changes may not be saved" line — we can't say "your photo hasn't been sent yet".
  • 👆 It only shows after real interaction. Browsers require the user to have interacted with the page — uploading a photo counts, so that's fine here.
  • 📱 Mobile is patchy. iOS Safari historically doesn't fire beforeunload (tab swipes/close on mobile often don't prompt at all). Worth testing on the devices we care about before shipping.
  • 🤝 It's a speed bump, not a wall. One click on "Leave" and the photo's gone anyway — it's cheap insurance, not a guarantee.
